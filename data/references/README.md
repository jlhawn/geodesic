# Observational references

Each reference the scorecards compare the model with is a file here that records where it comes from: the data set with its DOI or URL and license, the period, the box, the mask, the definition (including the model quantity that matches it), and the script that wrote it. A row of a scorecard takes its target from one of these files, never from a number typed into a script.

Definitions that the earlier scorecards got wrong, as the Nov 7 audit found them:

- **Clear sky.** EBAF's `rsutcs` and `rlutcs` are the clear-sky fluxes of cloud-free areas. The model's clear-sky pass removes the clouds from every column, which is EBAF's total-region clear sky; Loeb et al. (2020) put that 2.2 W/m² lower in OLR and 0.5 W/m² higher in reflected sunlight. The CERES file's `rstcre` and `rltcre` are EBAF's effects against the total-region clear sky (annual −45.13 and 25.70 W/m², against −45.61 and 27.80 from the cloud-free fluxes); score the model against those.
- **SST period.** WOA23's `decav` (`data/woa_sst_monthly_1deg.bin`, which the ocean's initial state still uses) averages the atlas's decades and is centered near 1988. Score SST against the 1991–2020 normal here, 0.20 K warmer over 30S–30N.
- **Global surface temperature.** The 14.85 °C target had no source. The model's `surfaceT` is the skin on land and sea ice and the top ocean layer on open sea, so the reference takes ERA5's skin temperature on land (land-sea mask ≥ 0.5) and on sea ice, and its SST on open sea: 15.25 °C for the year, 13.24 in January to 17.15 in July. With 2 m air in place of the skin, the station records' definition, it is 15.20 °C; the skin less the air is +0.05 K for the year, −0.07 K in January to +0.16 K in August, since land skin runs below its air in winter (−0.36 K in January) and above it in summer (+0.46 K in July) and sea-ice skin runs 0.2–0.7 K above its air. Berkeley Earth's Land/Ocean climatology for 1991–2020 (2 m air on land and sea ice, SST elsewhere) is 14.76 °C, 0.44 K below ERA5's same definition: the data sets differ by more than the definitions do. The Berkeley file is not kept here, since ERA5 matches the model's quantity and Berkeley's CC BY-NC license limits use.
- **2 m temperature.** The 40–60°N land row compared the model's lowest layer, about 20 m up, with ERA5's 2 m air, from 2010–2019 and March–September only. The model keeps no 2 m diagnostic: score its lowest-layer air brought to 2 m along the surface-layer profile of the run's own exchange coefficients against `era5-fields-1991-2020.bin`'s `t2m`.
- **Equatorial stress and pressure.** The earlier rows sampled ERA5 on four days a month in 2007–2019. `era5-fields-1991-2020.bin` holds the monthly means of every hour, 1991–2020; score the run's own stress (its exchange, not a rebuilt one) and sea-level pressure against `metss` and `msl` on the model's sea cells.
- **Undercurrent.** `tao-adcp-equatorial-undercurrent.json` gives each mooring's profile as the mean of its twelve calendar-month means, so a record heavy in some seasons does not bias it: cores 0.50 m/s at 188 m (165°E), 0.70 at 153 m (170°W), 1.08 at 106 m (140°W) and 0.95 at 75 m (110°W).
- **Precipitation.** `gpcp-precip-1991-2020.bin` is the file the earlier scorecards read from scratch (byte for byte), now with its provenance: global mean 2.69 mm/day.
- **Sea-ice extent.** The passive-microwave index counts no lake ice. The model's extent must count sea cells only; its FLake ice set the Arctic's March bias to +0.42 Mkm² where the sea ice alone gives +0.07.
